About this publication

The Meridian View

The Meridian View is an independent publication covering geopolitics, history, technology, and the structures of power. We publish long-form analysis on the conflicts, power shifts, and historical forces shaping the world today.

We have no ideological agenda, no advertisers directing our coverage, and no institutional backing. Our only interest is clarity.

What We Cover

We don't follow the news cycle. We write about the things that matter structurally — why states fail, how power is actually distributed, what history explains about the present, and how technology is reshaping the balance of power between nations. If a story requires more than a headline to understand, that's the story we want to tell.

Geopolitics

State behavior, alliances, conflicts, and the balance of power between nations.

History

The events and decisions that created the world we live in.

Economics

Trade, currency, debt, and the financial structures that underpin power.

Power

How political power is gained, exercised, and lost.

Technology

How technology is reshaping warfare, geopolitics, and the global order.

How We Work

Our analysis is produced by The Meridian Desk, a small collective of researchers and writers. We use AI tools as part of our research and drafting process, which we disclose openly. Every piece is human-directed, human-edited, and held to the same standard of factual accuracy regardless of how it was produced.

We believe in being transparent about AI usage because the alternative — pretending it isn't happening across the media industry — serves nobody. What matters is the quality of the analysis, the accuracy of the facts, and the honesty of the framing. We hold ourselves to all three.

Our Standard

We try to write about contested and complex topics without pretending they are simple. We try to be honest about what is known, what is uncertain, and what is interpretation. We correct errors when we find them. We do not write to confirm any particular worldview.

The name reflects the purpose. A meridian is the point of highest clarity — where you can see the furthest in every direction. That is what we are trying to offer: the clearest possible view of how the world actually works.
